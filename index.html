THE BASICS

What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a digital asset that can be sent over a peer-to-peer network without a traditional bank processing each transfer.

₿

A digital asset

Bitcoin exists digitally and transactions are recorded on a public blockchain.

↔

Peer-to-peer transfers

Users can transfer Bitcoin between compatible wallets. Network fees and confirmation times vary.

↗

Market volatility

Bitcoin's market price can rise or fall quickly. No return is guaranteed, and losses are possible.

TRADING 101

Know the risks before you invest.

Trading means buying and selling an asset in an attempt to benefit from price movements. A chart cannot predict future prices, and profits are never certain.
